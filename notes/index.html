I build things, question things, teach things, and occasionally say the thing everybody else was thinking but wasn't going to say.

My work crosses money behavior, community care, critical thinking, rootwork, and education. These areas don't need to become one business or one philosophy to belong to the same person. My Work

Three distinct bodies of work. Related because they share a founder, not because they're the same thing.

Money Behavior Strategy™

Knowing what to do with your money and actually doing it aren't the same thing.

I'm a Money Behavior Strategist and the founder of Money Behavior Strategy™, where I help people figure out what's getting in the way of the money plan they already know they need — and build one they can actually follow.

Explore Money Behavior Strategy

Now Alternative Soulutions

Dignity. Empowerment. Access.

I'm the founder and executive director of Now Alternative Soulutions, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit serving Kern County through four community programs — period products, hygiene essentials, family support, and financial education — delivered with no judgment and no red tape.

Explore Now Alternative Soulutions

Rukiyah The Rich Witch

Rootwork, cartomancy, and eleven years of lineage-based practice.

I'm a lineage-trained rootworker, herbalist, and cartomancer. This work is old — older than either business above — and it runs on its own tradition, not on money-behavior logic or nonprofit language.
