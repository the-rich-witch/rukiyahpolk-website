About Rukiyah Polk · Money Habit Coach

I help people change the habits behind their money choices.

For more than fifteen years, I have worked in money education and community support. Again and again, I saw people learn the right information and still struggle to use it in daily life.

I created Money Behavior Strategy™ to help people understand what to do with their money — and actually do it.

The framework helps you see your patterns, understand what keeps them going, and build a simple plan that makes the new choice easier to repeat.
