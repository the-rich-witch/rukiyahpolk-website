About Rukiyah Polk · Money Behavior Strategist

I got tired of money advice that stops at telling people what they should do.

Some people were never taught what to do with their money in the first place. Others know what they want to do — but what actually happens is different.

That gap is where I work.

It’s a big part of why I became a Money Behavior Strategist™.

I’m a money educator, but I don’t just teach the rule. I help people look at what they wanted their money to do, what actually happened, and what can change next time.

My Story

I had to learn what to do with my money, too.

There was a time when I was broke and homeless. I was a mom of six who had slept in shelters and depended on other people just to get by.

Later, I had income coming in, but having money and knowing what to do with it were not the same thing.

In 2009, I divided my income into four parts: 70% to LIVE, 10% to SAVE, 10% to GROW, and 10% to GIVE.

That meant all the money in my account was not there to spend. Each part had a job.

That changed how I used my money. It also made me pay attention to what happened between making a plan and actually following it.

The Work I Do Today

I didn’t arrive here from one direction.

I bring together money education, financial health counseling, credit, trauma-informed money work, behavior change, and lived experience.

But I don’t use that training to hand you another list of things you already know you “should” be doing.

I use it to help you figure out what happens between what you wanted your money to do and what actually happened.

That work became Money Behavior Strategy™: SEE what happened, ASK WHY it happened, TRY something different, and MAKE A PLAN you can actually use.

When You and I Work Together

We start with what actually happened to your money.

Maybe you planned to save $100 and spent it.

Maybe you keep checking your balance because you’re not sure what you can safely spend.

Maybe you make more money than you used to, but somehow there still isn’t much left.

That’s where I work.

We look at what money you had to work with, what you wanted it to do, what actually happened, and what was going on when you made those choices.

Then we decide what you can try differently the next time you face the same kind of money decision.

We don’t stop at “make a budget.” We make a plan you can actually follow in real life.
