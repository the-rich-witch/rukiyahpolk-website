21 Days of Paying Attention

One small thing to notice, question, or try each day.

Notice what happens. Get curious about it. Look again.

Start Paying Attention

Before we decide what something means, we can look at what actually happened.

We move quickly from what happened to what we think it means. From what we did to what that says about us. From what we want to what we think we should want.

For 21 days, we'll slow that down a little.

Some days I'll ask you to notice something. Some days I'll give you a question. Some days we'll try something small and see what happens.

You don't need to arrive with a problem to solve. You don't need to become a different person by Day 21. We're paying attention long enough to see what information is actually there.

What we'll do

NOTICE → QUESTION → TRY → LOOK AGAIN

NOTICE

Catch what happened before rushing to explain it.

QUESTION

Get specific about the story, conclusion, or assumption.

TRY

Change one small thing and see what happens.

LOOK AGAIN

Return with more information and see what you notice now.
