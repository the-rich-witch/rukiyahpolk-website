# Cashvertising Online website pass — August 2026

This build uses the completed Cashvertising Online notes as a decision framework, not a reason to redesign everything.

## Changes made
- Preserved the existing homepage positioning and overall brand.
- Standardized the framework to: SEE → ASK WHY → TRY → MAKE A PLAN.
- Replaced the homepage's hypothetical outcome section with a concrete method demonstration.
- Added an early CTA and clearer benefit/reassurance copy on the Work With Me page.
- Added a mobile-safe comparison table for the $247 Session vs. $597 Founding Client Experience.
- Made the Mini Money Binder a prominent part of the Founding Client experience and framed it as included, not as a cheap/free bonus.
- Added clear Founding Client ownership language around the binder and My Money Playbook™.
- Added real-person credibility/support using Rukiyah's photo and current credentials already represented on the site.
- Added a practical FAQ to reduce uncertainty before payment.
- Added a consumer-advocacy checklist: what to ask before paying anyone for money help.
- Added direct email contact paths so visitors do not hit a dead end.
- Replaced the nonfunctional “Founding Enrollment Coming Soon” button with a real email action until the Square checkout URL is ready.
- Standardized “Money Behavior Strategist” in places that still said “Money Habit Coach.”
- Added Email Rukiyah to site footers where appropriate.

## Intentionally NOT added
- No manufactured testimonials, statistics, guarantees, scarcity, or urgency.
- No fake Founding Client checkout URL.
- No “FREE Mini Money Binder” framing.
- No chat bot yet; the FAQ/support policy should exist before a bot is trained on it.
- No invented post-purchase response-time promise.
