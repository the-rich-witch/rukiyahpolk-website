Week 1 · SEE

See what’s actually happening.

Use your real numbers. Write down what came in, what you paid, what you bought, what you saved, and when the money started getting low.

Week 2 · ASK WHY

Find out what happens before you spend.

Pick a real money choice. What happened? What did you need or want? What were you thinking and feeling? What did you do with the money? What happened after?

Week 3 · TRY

Do one thing differently with real money.

Use LIVE, SAVE, GROW, and GIVE with your real numbers. Change one money choice and see what happens before the next session.

Week 4 · MAKE A PLAN

Make a plan you can actually follow.

Use what you learned in the first three weeks to decide what your money needs to do, what tends to throw you off, and what you will do when that happens. Put it into My Money Playbook™ so you can use it again.

Questions before you pay

Get the answer before you make the decision.

Which option should I choose?

If you have one clear money situation you want help with, start with the $247 session. If the same money problem keeps coming back and you want time to test changes in real life, the four-week experience is the better fit.

Are you going to tell me what I can and can’t spend money on?

No. We look at what’s happening in your real life and build a plan you understand and can actually use. The goal is to understand what’s happening, so you can build something that works for you.

Do I need a perfect budget before we start?

No. You don’t need to clean anything up first. Bring what’s actually happening. That’s what we work with.

What happens after I book the $247 session?

You’ll receive the booking confirmation and the intake steps connected to your appointment. Come with one money problem you want help working through.

When does the Founding Client Pilot happen?

The pilot officially launches September 22, 2026, on the autumn equinox. It’s limited to 8 Founding Clients, and each client completes four private one-on-one sessions on their own schedule. Enrollment closes September 20 or when all 8 spots are filled.

What happens after I submit the Founding Client form?

I’ll personally set up your Founding Client account in Square and send your $597 payment instructions. Your spot is saved after payment is complete. Then I’ll send your welcome steps, Starting Point information, and details about your Money Behavior Strategy™ kit before Week 1.

Can I ask a question before I pay?

Yes. Email rukiyah@rukiyahpolk.com. You don’t have to guess your way through the decision.

Before you pay anyone to help you with money

Ask a few questions first.

No matter who you work with, you should understand what you’re paying for and whether the help fits your real life.

01

Do they only tell me what I “should” do, or help me understand why the same problem keeps happening?

02

Does the plan fit my actual income, responsibilities, and life?

03

Will I leave with something I can realistically use after the conversation ends?

04

Are the promises clear and believable, without guaranteed miracle outcomes?

No matter who you choose, ask these questions first.
