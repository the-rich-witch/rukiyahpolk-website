5 Ways We React to Money

Money habits can have a reason.

You see the bill and put it back down. Someone needs help, so your plan changes. Extra money comes in and suddenly everything feels urgent. You tell yourself you'll deal with it next payday.

What you keep doing with money usually has a reason. First, notice what keeps happening.

01

The Avoider

"I will look at it later."

You step away when money feels too heavy.
02

The Firefighter

"I will handle the next emergency."

You react quickly but rarely get room to plan.
03

The Ghost

"I shut down when money comes up."

You disconnect when the pressure rises.
04

The Over-Giver

"Everyone else comes first."

You care for others while your own needs wait.
05

The Guard

"I need to control every dollar."

You hold tightly because letting go feels unsafe.

See the method in action

“I keep moving money back out of savings.”

A rule might say, “Save more.” Money Behavior Strategy™ starts with what actually happened and builds from there.

01SEE

$200 goes into savings on payday. Before the next payday, $150 comes back out.

02ASK WHY

Checking does not have enough to cover what life really costs between paydays.

03TRY

Instead of forcing $200 into savings, test $100 and watch what happens.

04MAKE A PLAN

Use the amount that works in real life, then adjust as your situation changes.

The goal is not to win at budgeting. It is to build a plan you can keep using.
