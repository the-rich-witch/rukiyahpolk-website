Pay attention. Get specific.

I pay attention to what's actually happening — not what's supposed to happen.

This is where I write, teach, question things, and share my work around money, behavior, community, relationships, and magick.

Try a Reflection
I build things, question things, teach things, and occasionally say the thing everybody else was thinking but wasn't going to say. ✦

I kept noticing the same thing in different places: people could know what they were supposed to do and still do something else.

I've seen it while teaching hygiene. I've seen it in credit and money work. I've seen it in relationships. I've seen it in myself.

Eventually, I stopped asking why people didn't know better. I started asking what happened between knowing what to do and actually doing it.

That's usually where my questions begin.

A way of looking, practiced

21 Days of Paying Attention

For 21 days, I'll send you one small thing to notice, question, or try.

We're not starting with what you need to fix. We're starting with what actually happens.

Notice what happens. Get curious about it. Look again.
