Why My Money Doesn’t Last

You got paid. So why didn’t the money last?

You were going to pay the bills. Save some. Make the money last until the next check.

Then the money started going out. Food. Gas. Bills. Things you wanted. Helping somebody. A few small buys you did not plan for.

Now the money is low, and payday is still days away.

Start with the last check

Let’s look at where the money went.

How much did you get?

What had to be paid?

What did you spend?

What was left?

When did you notice you were running short?

We’re not guessing. We’re looking at the money that came in and what happened to it.

Now look at the choices

What was going on when you spent the money?

Some money went to things you needed. Some went to things you wanted. You may have helped someone, treated yourself, spent more than you planned, or bought something because another check was coming.

Now look at what you were telling yourself when you spent it.

“I can replace it next payday.”

“I work hard. I want this.”

“They need my help.”

“It’s only a few dollars.”

Those thoughts matter because what you think about money can change what you do with it.

That’s part of your money mindset: what you learned to think and believe about money.

Your next payday

This time, give the money a job before you spend it.

Start with the amount that came in.

Set aside money for the bills that must be paid before the next check.

Set aside money for food, gas, and everyday needs.

Choose how much you can spend on things you want.

Put some money away for later.

Now you know what the money needs to do before you start spending it.

Money Behavior Strategy™

We work with a real check, not a perfect example.

We use four simple steps.

  1. 01SEE

    Write down how much came in, what you paid, what you bought, and what was left.

  2. 02ASK WHY

    Look at the choices that used the money. What did you need? What did you want? What were you thinking when you spent it?

  3. 03TRY

    Pick one thing to do differently next time. Move savings first. Set a spending amount. Hold money for a bill that comes later.

  4. 04MAKE A PLAN

    Decide how much goes to bills, everyday needs, spending, saving, and growing before the money starts going out.

You can start a new cycle

Your next check doesn’t have to disappear the same way.

You can learn what to do with your money when you get it.
