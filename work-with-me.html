Questions before you pay

Get the answer before you make the decision.

Which option should I choose?

If you have one clear money situation you want help with, start with the $247 session. If the same kinds of money choices keep repeating and you want time to test changes in real life, the four-week experience is the better fit.

Are you going to tell me what I can and cannot spend money on?

No. We look at what is happening in your real life and build a plan you understand and can actually use. The goal is not to judge your spending.

Do I need a perfect budget before we start?

No. You do not need to clean anything up first. Bring what is actually happening. That is what we work with.

What happens after I book the $247 session?

You’ll receive the booking confirmation and the intake steps connected to your appointment. Come with one money problem you want help working through.

What happens after I join the Founding Client Experience?

You’ll receive your welcome steps, Starting Point information, and details about your Money Behavior Strategy™ kit before Week 1.

Can I ask a question before I pay?

Yes. Email rukiyah@rukiyahpolk.com. You do not have to guess your way through the decision.

Before you pay anyone to help you with money

Ask a few questions first.

No matter who you work with, you should understand what you are paying for and whether the help fits your real life.

01

Do they only tell me what I “should” do, or help me understand why the same problem keeps happening?

02

Does the plan fit my actual income, responsibilities, and life?

03

Will I leave with something I can realistically use after the conversation ends?

04

Are the promises clear and believable, without guaranteed miracle outcomes?

No matter who you choose, ask these questions first.
