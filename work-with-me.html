Week 1 · SEE

See what is actually happening.

Look at where your money goes, what happens around payday, and the choices that keep repeating. You’ll also use 70/10/10/10 as a simple way to see and compare.

Week 2 · ASK WHY

Understand what makes the pattern make sense.

We’ll look at what happens right before the behavior, what makes one choice easier than another, and what may be getting in the way.

Week 3 · TRY

Try one real change.

You’ll test one small change with your real life and real money. What works gives us direction. What doesn’t work gives us information.

Week 4 · MAKE A PLAN

Build what you can keep using.

We’ll review what you learned and build My Money Playbook™ — your personal guide for what works, what gets in the way, and what you want to keep doing.

Questions before you pay

Get the answer before you make the decision.

Which option should I choose?

If you have one clear money situation you want help with, start with the $247 session. If the same kinds of money choices keep repeating and you want time to test changes in real life, the four-week experience is the better fit.

Are you going to tell me what I can and can’t spend money on?

No. We look at what’s happening in your real life and build a plan you understand and can actually use. The goal is to understand what’s happening, so you can build something that works for you.

Do I need a perfect budget before we start?

No. You don’t need to clean anything up first. Bring what’s actually happening. That’s what we work with.

What happens after I book the $247 session?

You’ll receive the booking confirmation and the intake steps connected to your appointment. Come with one money problem you want help working through.

When does the Founding Client Pilot happen?

The pilot officially launches September 22, 2026, on the autumn equinox. It’s limited to 8 Founding Clients, and each client completes four private one-on-one sessions on their own schedule. Enrollment closes September 20 or when all 8 spots are filled.

What happens after I join the Founding Client Pilot?

You’ll receive your welcome steps, Starting Point information, and details about your Money Behavior Strategy™ kit before Week 1.

Can I ask a question before I pay?

Yes. Email rukiyah@rukiyahpolk.com. You don’t have to guess your way through the decision.

Before you pay anyone to help you with money

Ask a few questions first.

01

02

Does the plan fit my actual income, responsibilities, and life?

03

Are the promises clear and believable, without guaranteed miracle outcomes?
